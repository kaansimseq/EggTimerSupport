# Egg Timer Support

Welcome to the support page for the **Egg Timer** app! 🍳

Here you'll find frequently asked questions, known issues, and ways to contact us.

---

## 📱 About the App

**Egg Timer** is a simple and beautiful app that helps you boil your eggs just the way you like them:  
**Liquid, Soft, or Hard** — just pick the type, size, and temperature. We'll notify you when it's ready!

---

## ❓ FAQ

### Q: The timer doesn't run in background.  
A: Please make sure background activity is allowed in your device settings.

### Q: I don't receive notifications.  
A: Ensure notifications are enabled for Egg Timer in your phone settings.
